    About this model

    ▾

    Vertebral column: 7 cervical + 12 thoracic + 5 lumbar + 1 sacrum + 1 coccyx = 26. In this 3D model the vertebrae are fused into one piece per region, and each toe’s metatarsal and phalanges form one piece, so they are labelled as groups rather than split by guesswork. The hyoid and the ear ossicles are not shown. Teeth are shown but are not bones.

    Joint markers are placed on the subject’s left side; the subject faces you in Front view, so their left is on your right. Hindi names follow NCERT Hindi-medium terms.
