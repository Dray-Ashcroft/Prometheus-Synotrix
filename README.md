## 📖 About

An interactive 3D simulator for teaching how the human skeleton is built and how its joints move. Real bone models let students see:

- **how joints are classified** by the movement they allow
- **what is inside a joint**: bones, cartilage, ligaments, discs and synovial fluid
- **where each bone sits** in the 206-bone skeleton, and which division it belongs to

Aligned with NCERT for Class IX and XI. Built for classroom smart panels; it works on phones too.

## ✦ Two Tabs

Switch tabs with the two icons at the top: the knee icon for joints, the skull icon for the skeleton.

| Tab | What it shows |
|:--|:--|
| 🦵 **Types of Joints** | A joint classification chart that opens a 3D scene, description, examples and allowed movement for each joint type |
| 💀 **Human Skeleton** | The full 3D skeleton with bone identification, axial and appendicular filters, joint markers, Hindi names and a quiz |

### 🦵 Types of Joints

- Tap **INITIATE**, then choose a branch of the chart:

| Branch | Type | Further choices |
|:--|:--|:--|
| 🔴 **Fixed** | Synarthrosis | — |
| 🟡 **Slightly movable** | Amphiarthrosis | — |
| 🟢 **Freely movable** | Diarthrosis (synovial) | Ball & Socket · Pivot · Hinge |

- Each type opens a **3D diagram** with three cards: **Description**, **Examples** and **Movement Allowed**.
- Turn on **Inspect**, then tap a structure in the 3D scene to see its name and a short description, such as the nucleus pulposus of a disc, the glenoid labrum, the acetabulum or the transverse ligament.

### 💀 Human Skeleton

- **Tap any bone** to identify it. A card shows its name and description, with **Focus** to zoom in on it.
- Choose the **Front, Side or Back** camera view.
- Filter the skeleton by division: **All · Axial (80) · Appendicular (126) · Regions**. Regions colours the skeleton by body region and shows a legend.
- Gold **joint markers** sit on real joints. Tap one for its description, then **Open 3D joint →** to jump straight to that joint in the Types of Joints tab. Use the **Joints** toggle to show or hide them.
- **हिंदी** switches bone names between English and Hindi.
- **★ Quiz** is a 10-question find-the-bone game with a score.

## 🦿 Joint Classification

| Type | Examples in the simulator |
|:--|:--|
| **Fixed (fibrous)** | Skull sutures (coronal, sagittal, lambdoid, squamous) · Gomphosis (tooth in socket) |
| **Slightly movable (cartilaginous)** | Between vertebrae · Pubic symphysis · First rib–sternum |
| **Freely movable (synovial)** | Shoulder · Hip · Knee · Elbow · Wrist · Ankle |
| ↳ Ball & socket | Shoulder · Hip |
| ↳ Pivot | Atlas–axis |
| ↳ Hinge | Elbow · Knee |

**Joint markers on the skeleton:** skull sutures, atlas–axis, shoulder, elbow, intervertebral joint, pubic symphysis, hip and knee. Limb joints are marked on the subject's left side; the subject faces you in Front view, so their left is on your right.

## 🩻 Skeleton Divisions

| Division | Bones |
|:--|:--|
| **Axial skeleton** | **80**: skull 29 (cranial 8, facial 14, hyoid 1, ear ossicles 6) · vertebral column 26 · sternum 1 · ribs 24 |
| **Appendicular skeleton** | **126**: upper limbs 60 · lower limbs 60 · pectoral girdle 4 · pelvic girdle 2 |

| Area | Concepts |
|:--|:--|
| Classification | Fixed, slightly movable and freely movable joints · Synarthrosis, amphiarthrosis, diarthrosis |

## 🧭 Controls

| Where | Action | What it does |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Joints tab | Left-click drag · Right-click drag · Scroll | Rotate · Pan · Zoom the 3D joint |
| Joints tab | **Inspect** toggle, then tap | Name and describe a structure |
| Skeleton tab | Drag · Pinch or scroll · Right-drag | Rotate · Zoom · Pan |
| Skeleton tab | Tap a bone | Identify it |
| Skeleton tab | Front · Side · Back | Change the camera view |
| Skeleton tab | All · Axial · Appendicular · Regions | Filter the skeleton |
| Skeleton tab | **Joints** · **हिंदी** · **★ Quiz** | Markers · language · quiz |

## ⚠️ Educational Disclaimer

Models are simplified for educational purposes and are not precise clinical references. In the skeleton model:

- the vertebrae are fused into one piece per region
- each toe's metatarsal and phalanges form one piece, so they are labelled as groups
- the hyoid and the ear ossicles are not shown
- teeth are shown but are not bones

Hindi names follow NCERT Hindi-medium terms.
