## About

An interactive 3D simulator of the human skeleton and its joints. Real bone models show how joints are classified, what is inside a joint, and where each of the 206 bones sits. Aligned with NCERT Class IX and XI.

Built for classroom smart panels and phones.

## Explore

| Section | What You See | Tap to Explore |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Types of Joints** | Classification chart that opens a rigged 3D model of each joint, with description, examples and movement | Fixed · Slightly movable · Freely movable → Ball and socket · Pivot · Hinge · Inspect any structure |
| **Human Skeleton** | Full 3D skeleton: Axial 80 and Appendicular 126 bones | Any bone · Axial / Appendicular / Regions filters · Gold joint markers · Hindi names · 10-question quiz |

**Concepts:** joint classification · synarthrosis, amphiarthrosis, diarthrosis · synovial joint structure · axial and appendicular skeleton · body regions · bone identification · range of movement

## Science

| Joint | Also Called | Joined By | Movement | Examples | 3D Model |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **Fixed** | Fibrous · Synarthrosis | Dense fibrous tissue | None | Skull sutures · tooth in socket (gomphosis) | Skull with sutures and a tooth socket |
| **Slightly movable** | Cartilaginous · Amphiarthrosis | Cartilage | Limited; absorbs shock | Between vertebrae · pubic symphysis · first rib–sternum | L3–L4 vertebrae and disc |
| **Freely movable** | Synovial · Diarthrosis | Fluid-filled cavity, cartilage, capsule | Free | Shoulder · hip · knee · elbow · wrist · ankle | Shoulder |
| ↳ Ball and socket | Multiaxial | Rounded head in a cup-shaped socket | All directions, including rotation | Shoulder · hip | Hip |
| ↳ Pivot | Uniaxial | One bone turning around another | Rotation | Atlas–axis (neck) · radius and ulna | Atlas–axis |
| ↳ Hinge | Uniaxial | Like a door hinge | Flexion and extension | Elbow · knee · ankle · fingers | Elbow |

<details>
<summary><b>Structures you can inspect</b></summary>

| Model | Named Structures |
|:--|:--|
| Cartilaginous (L3–L4) | Lumbar vertebrae L3 and L4 · Intervertebral disc · Nucleus pulposus |
| Synovial (shoulder) | Humerus · Scapula · Clavicle · Glenoid cavity · Glenoid labrum · Articular cartilage · Joint capsule · Synovial membrane · Synovial fluid · Coracohumeral and glenohumeral ligaments |
| Ball and socket (hip) | Femur · Hip bone · Acetabulum · Acetabular labrum · Articular cartilage · Joint capsule · Iliofemoral, pubofemoral and ischiofemoral ligaments |
| Hinge (elbow) | Humerus · Trochlea · Capitulum · Ulna · Trochlear notch · Olecranon process · Radius · Articular cartilage · Ligament |
| Pivot (atlas–axis) | Atlas (C1) · Axis (C2) · Third cervical vertebra (C3) · Transverse ligament · Articular cartilage |

</details>

|:--|:--|

| Appendicular · 126 | Upper limbs 60 · Lower limbs 60 · Pectoral girdle 4 · Pelvic girdle 2 |

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Educational note</b></summary>

Models are simplified for teaching and are not clinical references. Vertebrae are fused per region, each toe is one piece, the hyoid and ear ossicles are not shown, and teeth are shown but are not bones. Hindi names follow NCERT Hindi-medium terms.

</details>

## Controls

| Control | Function |
|:--|:--|
| Drag · Pinch or scroll · Right-drag | Rotate · Zoom · Pan |
| Inspect, then tap | Name a structure in a 3D joint |
| Tap a bone · Focus | Identify a bone · Zoom to it |
| Front · Side · Back | Skeleton views |
| Gold markers · Open 3D joint | Jump from the skeleton to the matching joint |
| Hindi · Quiz | Hindi bone names · Find-the-bone quiz |
