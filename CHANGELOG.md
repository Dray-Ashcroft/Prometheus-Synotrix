## RC V — 2026-10-22

**Human Skeleton becomes a full learning tool.**

### Added
- **Bone identification:** tap any bone for its name and description, with **Focus** to zoom in.
- **Division filters:** All · Axial (80) · Appendicular (126) · Regions.
- **Joint markers:** gold markers on real joints that open the matching 3D joint in the Types of Joints tab.
- **हिंदी:** bone names in Hindi, following NCERT Hindi-medium terms.
- **Quiz:** a 10-question find-the-bone game with a score.
- **Explore Human Skeleton:** press-and-hold entry from the Joints tab.
- New banner and screenshots.

## RC I — 2026-08-08

**Initial public release.**

### Added
- **Types of Joints:** a classification chart (Fixed · Slightly movable · Freely movable → Ball & Socket · Pivot · Hinge) opening 3D joints with description, examples and movement.
- **Inspect:** tap a structure in a 3D joint to name and describe it.
- **Human Skeleton** tab with Front, Side and Back views.
- Anatomy models from BodyParts3D.
